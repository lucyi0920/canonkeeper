# Episode 2 — Whispers of the Obsidian Key

## Panel 1
**Visual:** Extreme close-up flash-forward in the dim, candlelit depths of the Archive Vault. Mira, a young woman with long silver-white hair and bright teal eyes, secretly tucks the silver Bell of Aethelgard—an intricate, palm-sized silver chime etched with ancient sealing runes—deep into the inner pocket of her white-and-teal rune cloak. Her delicate fingers tremble slightly against the silver metal, while the polished wooden shaft of her intact mage staff rests against her shoulder. Shadows obscure the background, illuminated only by a cold cyan glow radiating faintly from the bell's runes.
**Dialogue:**
Archivist Orin: "Pray you never have reason to ring it, child. Its toll is absolute."

## Panel 2
**Visual:** Wide establishing shot transitioning back to thirty minutes earlier. The grand gothic spires of Starfall Academy tower against a bruised twilight sky, where twin moons hang behind wispy violet clouds. Far below the central courtyard, a spiraling stone staircase descends into the subterranean bedrock toward the Archive Vault, lit by floating spheres of pale blue astral fire.
**Dialogue:**
Narration: "Thirty minutes earlier. Beneath the Seventh Spire of Starfall Academy."
Narration: "Few cadets ever see the lower vaults. Fewer still are summoned there on the night of their combat trial."

## Panel 3
**Visual:** Medium two-shot of Jin and Mira walking down the ancient spiral stairs. Jin has tousled jet-black hair, piercing golden-amber eyes, and a completely smooth, unscarred face on both the viewer's left and viewer's right cheeks. He wears his high-collared dark navy astral coat with silver embroidery, and strapped to his waist is the newly awakened Starfall Blade, with Mira's bright teal ribbon tied securely around the hilt. Beside him, Mira walks with her wooden mage staff in hand, her white-and-teal rune cloak billowing slightly in the draft.
**Dialogue:**
Jin: "Seven hundred steps and counting. Did we pass the trial in the arena today, or are they banishing us to the dungeons?"
Mira: "Stop complaining, Jin. Your Starfall Blade literally split the arena barrier in half during the trial. It's a miracle the High Council didn't lock you in an iron cell."

## Panel 4
**Visual:** Close-up on Jin from the front. His golden-amber eyes glint with playful defiance, and both cheeks are completely smooth and unscarred. He rests one hand casually on the pommel of the Starfall Blade, where the loose ends of Mira's teal ribbon flutter against his dark navy astral coat.
**Dialogue:**
Jin: "Hey, the sword woke up on its own! Besides, your ribbon makes a great grip wrap. Balanced the weight right out."
Mira (off-panel): "That ribbon was woven from consecrated star-silk, you idiot! It wasn't meant for your sweaty palms!"

## Panel 5
**Visual:** Medium shot at the base of the stairs, where two massive bronze doors engraved with constellations stand ajar. Standing guard in front of the doors is Instructor Kaelen, Commander of the Astral Vanguard, an imposing veteran in gleaming silver-and-blue plate armor with a heavy broadsword strapped across his back and a stern, weathered face.
**Dialogue:**
Instructor Kaelen: "Enough chatter, cadets. Your voices echo down the entire shaft."
Jin: "Commander Kaelen! You're guarding the library now?"

## Panel 6
**Visual:** Close-up on Instructor Kaelen's stern face, lit from below by the blue astral lanterns. His eyes narrow as he looks down at Jin and Mira, his gauntleted hand resting on his belt.
**Dialogue:**
Instructor Kaelen: "This isn't a library, cadet. It's the Sealed Reliquary of the First Era. The Astral Vanguard has been placed on high alert since your blade resonated with the sky-fault this afternoon."
Instructor Kaelen: "And watch your tone inside. Elder Vane is already speaking with the Archivist."

## Panel 7
**Visual:** Medium shot as Jin and Mira exchange a quick glance. Mira's bright teal eyes widen slightly with apprehension, clutching her wooden mage staff tighter against her white-and-teal rune cloak. Jin's unscarred face turns serious, his tousled jet-black hair falling over his brow.
**Dialogue:**
Mira: "Elder Vane of the High Council? Why would he come all the way down to the subterranean vaults?"
Instructor Kaelen: "Go inside and find out. Orin is waiting."

## Panel 8
**Visual:** Grand wide shot of the interior of the Archive Vault. Colossal stone bookshelves reach up into darkness, crammed with leather-bound tomes, crystal memory cylinders, and ancient stone tablets. In the center of the circular chamber stands a wide stone table illuminated by hovering brass astrolabes. Two figures stand near the table: Archivist Orin, a hunched, frail old scholar with milky-white spectacles and ink-stained robes, and Elder Vane, a tall, gaunt aristocrat dressed in sweeping High Council dark robes trimmed with obsidian thread.
**Dialogue:**
Elder Vane: "You are too sentimental, Orin. The boy is an unproven variable, and the girl is barely an initiate of the Third Circle."
Archivist Orin: "And yet the Starfall Blade chose him, Elder Vane. Just as the stars foretold three centuries ago."

## Panel 9
**Visual:** Medium shot of Elder Vane turning toward the entrance as Jin and Mira step through the bronze doors. Elder Vane's face is sharp and aristocratic, shadowed by the high collar of his High Council dark robes. One of his pale hands is tucked inside the folds of his dark robe, where the faint, angular silhouette of a hidden object—the Obsidian Key—presses subtly against the fabric over his chest.
**Dialogue:**
Elder Vane: "Speak of the anomalies, and they appear."
Elder Vane: "Cadet Jin. Cadet Mira. I trust your wounds from the combat trial have been tended to?"

## Panel 10
**Visual:** Medium two-shot of Jin and Mira bowing respectfully. Jin's face is completely smooth and unscarred on both the viewer's left and right sides; his dark navy astral coat gleams with silver embroidery under the astrolabe light. Mira inclines her silver-white head, her wooden mage staff held vertically at her side.
**Dialogue:**
Mira: "Yes, Elder Vane. Thank you for your grace."
Jin: "Not a scratch on me, sir. Though the arena floorboards might need a mason."

## Panel 11
**Visual:** Close-up on Elder Vane looking down his nose at Jin, his eyes cold and calculating. He brushes past them toward the heavy bronze doors, his High Council dark robes trailing behind him like liquid shadow.
**Dialogue:**
Elder Vane: "Arrogance has buried better swordsmen than you, boy. Remember that the High Council watches all who wield relics of the First Era."
Elder Vane: "Archivist Orin, see that this consultation is brief. The Council convenes at midnight."

## Panel 12
**Visual:** Over-the-shoulder shot from behind Jin and Mira as Elder Vane exits past Instructor Kaelen into the shadowed stairwell. Jin watches him leave, his golden-amber eyes narrowed suspiciously, his unscarred face tense.
**Dialogue:**
Jin: "Warm fellow. Does he ever smile, or would his face crack?"
Archivist Orin (off-panel): "Pay Elder Vane no mind, young Jin. Fear makes hard men out of even the wisest councilors."

## Panel 13
**Visual:** Medium shot of Archivist Orin hobbling around the central stone table, leaning on a gnarled brass cane. Despite his age, his eyes behind his thick spectacles are sharp and urgent. He gestures toward a stack of oversized parchment scrolls spread across the stone surface.
**Dialogue:**
Archivist Orin: "Come closer, both of you. Lock the inner ward behind you, Mira. What I am about to show you has been forbidden to cadet eyes for three hundred years."

## Panel 14
**Visual:** Medium shot of Mira tapping the butt of her intact wooden mage staff against a glowing rune Circle on the floor near the doors. A shimmering veil of teal mana rises over the archway, soundproofing the vault. Her long silver-white hair floats slightly in the mana draft.
**Dialogue:**
Mira: "A silence ward? Archivist Orin, what is going on? Why did Jin's sword react like that during the trial today?"
Archivist Orin: "Because a blade forged to seal the Abyss only wakes when the Abyss begins to stir."

## Panel 15
**Visual:** High-angle close-up of the stone table as Archivist Orin unrolls three massive charcoal rubbings taken from ancient monoliths. The rubbings depict seven jagged scars tearing across a map of the continent, with monstrous, shadowy coils spilling from each tear and seven radiant champions standing before them.
**Dialogue:**
Archivist Orin: "Behold the rubbings of the First Cataclysm. Three centuries ago, the fabric of our realm tore open at seven convergence points—the Seven Abyssal Rifts."
Jin (off-panel): "We learned about the Rifts in primary history. The First Saint and the Astral Monarchs sealed them forever."

## Panel 16
**Visual:** Close-up on Archivist Orin's weathered hand pointing a trembling, ink-stained finger at the largest, central tear on the charcoal rubbing—labeled in ancient script as 'Vaelen Temple'—and another tear to the far north labeled 'Northgate Citadel'.
**Dialogue:**
Archivist Orin: "'Forever' is a lie told to help children sleep, Jin. No seal is eternal. Over three hundred years, the astral chains binding the Seven Rifts have corroded."
Archivist Orin: "Last month, the monolith at the Sunken Citadel of Northgate went dark. And today, when you drew the Starfall Blade, every seismograph in this vault registered a tremor beneath Vaelen Temple."

## Panel 17
**Visual:** Medium two-shot of Jin and Mira reacting with shock. Jin leans over the table, his tousled jet-black hair falling forward, his smooth, unscarred face illuminated by the glowing astrolabe above. Mira presses a hand to the collar of her white-and-teal rune cloak, her bright teal eyes wide.
**Dialogue:**
Mira: "Vaelen Temple? That's less than three days' march from the Academy! If the Great Abyssal Rift there is weakening..."
Jin: "Then why isn't the High Council sending the Astral Vanguard to reinforce the seal right now?"

## Panel 18
**Visual:** Medium shot of Archivist Orin looking grimly toward the closed bronze doors where Elder Vane exited moments ago. The shadows of the towering bookshelves loom behind the old man.
**Dialogue:**
Archivist Orin: "Because the High Council is divided. Half of them refuse to believe the seals are failing, and someone within the upper spire has been systematically purging the Archive's records on rift-sealing."
Archivist Orin: "Jin, would you check the third bookshelf in the eastern alcove? Behind the ledger of the Northern Marches, there should be a bronze reliquary box. Bring it to me."

## Panel 19
**Visual:** Medium shot as Jin nods, turning away from the central table and walking into the dim aisle between the colossal stone bookshelves on the viewer's right. The teal ribbon on the hilt of his Starfall Blade sways against his dark navy astral coat as he heads into the shadows.
**Dialogue:**
Jin: "Third shelf, eastern alcove. On it."
Archivist Orin: "Take a floating lantern with you, boy. The dust back there is older than your grandfather."

## Panel 20
**Visual:** Tight two-shot at the central table once Jin is out of earshot. Archivist Orin reaches hurriedly into the deep sleeve of his scholar's robe and withdraws a small, exquisite object wrapped in velvet cloth. Mira watches him closely, her wooden mage staff resting against her shoulder, her silver-white hair framing her serious face.
**Dialogue:**
Mira: "Archivist Orin... there WAS no bronze box behind the third bookshelf, was there? You sent Jin away on purpose."
Archivist Orin: "Sharp as ever, child. Keep your voice down. What I have for you is a burden he must not know you carry."

## Panel 21
**Visual:** Extreme close-up of Archivist Orin's aged hands unwrapping the velvet cloth to reveal the Bell of Aethelgard—a delicate, palm-sized silver chime etched with intricate spiral runes that pulse with a faint, mournful cyan light.
**Dialogue:**
Archivist Orin: "This is the Bell of Aethelgard, forged by the First Saint herself at the close of the Cataclysm."
Mira (off-panel): "It's... radiating pure astral resonance. I've never felt an artifact like this."

## Panel 22
**Visual:** Medium close-up of Archivist Orin pressing the silver Bell of Aethelgard into Mira's gloved hands. His expression behind his spectacles is agonized, almost pleading. Mira holds the silver chime carefully, its cyan glow reflecting in her bright teal eyes.
**Dialogue:**
Archivist Orin: "Listen to me carefully, Mira. The Starfall Blade can cleave abyssal fiends, but a sword alone cannot mend a shattered dimensional seal."
Archivist Orin: "If a Great Rift collapses completely—if the seal dies before the Vanguard can re-anchor it—only the Bell of Aethelgard can forge an instant, unbreakable barrier."

## Panel 23
**Visual:** Close-up on Mira's face, lit from below by the silver bell. Her brow furrows in realization as she studies the ancient blood-runes carved inside the rim of the chime.
**Dialogue:**
Mira: "An instant barrier of that magnitude... No mage's mana pool could fuel a spell like that. Not even an Archmage."
Mira: "Archivist... what powers the Bell?"

## Panel 24
**Visual:** Tight close-up on Archivist Orin's eyes behind his spectacles, heavy with sorrow and ancient dread.
**Dialogue:**
Archivist Orin: "Not mana, child. If rung inside a collapsing rift, the Bell of Aethelgard anchors the dying seal by consuming the caster's entire soul core."
Archivist Orin: "Body, spirit, and memory—shattered into starlight to become the lock itself. A soul consumed by the Bell can never be revived, nor summoned back from the beyond."

## Panel 25
**Visual:** Medium shot of Mira standing frozen, the silver Bell of Aethelgard resting heavy in her palms. Behind her, down the distant aisle of the third bookshelf, the faint blue glow of Jin's floating lantern bobs as he searches the shelves.
**Dialogue:**
Mira: "My entire soul core... Total annihilation."
Archivist Orin: "If you tell Jin what this chime requires, he will tear it from your hands and throw it into the sea. Or worse, he will try to ring it himself—and a swordsman without runic affinity would only die in vain without completing the seal."

## Panel 26
**Visual:** Close-up on Mira as she looks toward the distant aisle where Jin is searching, her bright teal eyes softening with quiet resolve and protective devotion. She closes her fingers firmly around the silver Bell of Aethelgard, muffling its faint chime, and slips it deep into the inner fold of her white-and-teal rune cloak.
**Dialogue:**
Mira: "He's reckless enough without knowing something like this exists. He already thinks he has to carry the whole sky on his shoulders."
Mira: "I'll keep it hidden, Archivist. If the worst comes... I won't let the Abyss take him."

## Panel 27
**Visual:** Medium shot in the dusty aisle behind the third bookshelf. Jin, his face completely smooth and unscarred on both cheeks, holds a floating blue lantern up to the stone wall behind a row of heavy leather ledgers. His golden-amber eyes narrow as he spots fresh scrape marks on the ancient masonry where books have been moved aside.
**Dialogue:**
Jin: "Hey, Old Man Orin! There's no bronze box back here, just a pile of moth-eaten tax ledgers!"
Jin: "Wait a second... what is this carved into the stone?"

## Panel 28
**Visual:** Extreme close-up of the stone wall behind the third bookshelf. Carved deep into the granite with a sharp, metallic point is an intricate circular cipher surrounding a stylized keyhole and jagged runic script that glows faintly with residual dark purple mana.
**Dialogue:**
Narration: "Carved into the foundation stone behind the third shelf—a High Council cipher, fresh enough that stone dust still clings to the grooves."
Jin (off-panel): "Mira! Orin! Come look at this!"

## Panel 29
**Visual:** Medium three-shot in the narrow bookshelf aisle as Mira and Archivist Orin hurry over to join Jin. Mira's white-and-teal rune cloak hangs normally, the silver Bell of Aethelgard now completely concealed inside it, her wooden mage staff held in her right hand. Jin points at the wall, his dark navy astral coat brushing the dusty shelves.
**Dialogue:**
Jin: "Look at the dust on the floor. Someone was back here recently, and they used a dagger or a key to gouge this cipher into the wall."
Mira: "Wait—nothing happened while you were back here, right? Did you touch the runes?"

## Panel 30
**Visual:** Close-up of Jin turning his head toward Mira with a sheepish grin, his face completely smooth and unscarred on both the viewer's left and viewer's right sides. Mira glares at him in exasperation.
**Dialogue:**
Jin: "Do I look like the kind of idiot who touches glowing purple curses in a forbidden basement?"
Mira: "Yes. Every single day."
Archivist Orin: "Hush, both of you! Let me see the script!"

## Panel 31
**Visual:** Over-the-shoulder shot as Archivist Orin leans close to the wall, adjusting his spectacles as the blue lantern illuminates the carved cipher. His face goes pale as he traces the air above the runic words without touching them.
**Dialogue:**
Archivist Orin: "By the First Star... This isn't a curse. It's a transcription log left by my predecessor before he vanished last winter."
Archivist Orin: "It reads: *'The Seventh Seal was not broken from within. The Obsidian Key of the Sunken Citadel of Northgate has been taken from the High Council Sanctum.'*"

## Panel 32
**Visual:** Close-up on Jin's serious, unscarred face, his golden-amber eyes reflecting the eerie purple glow of the carved cipher. His hand drops instinctively to the hilt of the Starfall Blade, his fingers brushing Mira's teal ribbon.
**Dialogue:**
Jin: "The Obsidian Key? What is that—some kind of reverse-seal?"
Archivist Orin (off-panel): "Worse. When the First Monarchs sealed the Seven Rifts, they forged a master key of black star-glass capable of unlocking the dimensional anchors—meant only to vent pressure over centuries."

## Panel 33
**Visual:** Medium shot of Archivist Orin stepping back from the bookshelf, his cane trembling against the stone floor. Mira stands beside him, gripping her wooden mage staff tightly, her expression grim.
**Dialogue:**
Archivist Orin: "If someone stole the Obsidian Key from the Sunken Citadel of Northgate and brought it here to the Academy... it means a traitor within the High Council itself is deliberately unsealing the Rifts!"
Mira: "A traitor on the High Council?! Then we have to warn Commander Kaelen right now!"

## Panel 34
**Visual:** Sudden dramatic close-up of the silence ward at the far end of the Archive Vault shattering like blue glass! A jagged black throwing dagger wreathed in purple shadow-smoke slices through the barrier and embeds itself deep into the stone table, inches from the charcoal rubbings.
**Dialogue:**
**SFX:** *SHATTER—THWACK!*
Jin: "Get down!"

## Panel 35
**Visual:** Dynamic action shot as Jin lunges in front of Mira and Archivist Orin, drawing the Starfall Blade in a brilliant arc of silver-blue starlight that deflects a second volley of black throwing daggers in mid-air. Sparks and purple poison droplets fly across the dark navy fabric of his astral coat; his face is fierce and completely unscarred, and Mira's teal ribbon streams behind the gleaming hilt.
**Dialogue:**
**SFX:** *CLANG-CLANG-TING!*
Jin: "Stay behind me! Those blades are coated in shadow-venom!"

## Panel 36
**Visual:** Low-angle shot looking up at the top of the third bookshelf. Crouched atop the towering stone cornice is a sleek assassin clad in muted black leather armor and wearing a snarling wooden shadow-hound mask with glowing red eye-slits. In his left hand, he clutches a rolled parchment stolen from the upper shelves; in his right, a curved ritual dagger dripping with dark purple venom.
**Dialogue:**
Shadow-Hound Assassin: "The Archivist talks too much... and the brat draws fast."
Jin: "Who sent you?! Get down here and answer my blade!"

## Panel 37
**Visual:** Wide kinetic shot as the assassin kicks off the top of the bookshelf, Hurtling toward the vault's exit doors in a blur of shadow-step magic. Mira raises her intact wooden mage staff with both hands, the crystal tip blazing with bright teal runic light as she casts a binding spell across the aisle.
**Dialogue:**
Mira: "You're not leaving this vault! *Astral Chain: Fourth Binding!*"
**SFX:** *FWOOSH!*

## Panel 38
**Visual:** Medium action shot near the bronze doors. Glowing teal chains erupt from the stone floor and wrap around the assassin's ankle, yanking him out of his shadow-step and slamming him onto the flagstones just inside the open doorway. Outside in the stairwell, Instructor Kaelen lies slumped against the wall, temporarily paralyzed by a dark sleep-dart in his neck.
**Dialogue:**
Mira: "Commander Kaelen!"
Archivist Orin: "It's a shadow-numbing dart—he's alive, just paralyzed! Don't let the assassin reach the stairs!"

## Panel 39
**Visual:** Dynamic close-up as the assassin slices through Mira's glowing teal mana-chains with his poisoned ritual dagger and springs up into the narrow spiral stairwell. Jin charges through the bronze doors right on his heels, the Starfall Blade blazing with white-gold astral fire in his grip, the teal ribbon whipping in the wind.
**Dialogue:**
Jin: "Mira, check on Kaelen! I've got this bastard!"
Mira (off-panel): "Jin, wait! Don't chase him into the blind turns alone!"

## Panel 40
**Visual:** Vertical bird's-eye shot looking down the dizzying spiral stairwell. Jin vaults over the stone banister, dropping down a full level to cut off the assassin's ascent. His dark navy astral coat billows around him like wings, his tousled jet-black hair flying back, his unscarred face locked in total concentration.
**Dialogue:**
**SFX:** *WHOOSH—SLAM!*
Jin: "End of the line, hound!"

## Panel 41
**Visual:** Intense close-up clash on the narrow stone steps! The assassin lunges downward with his curved, venom-dripping dagger aimed straight at Jin's throat. Jin brings the Starfall Blade up in a two-handed diagonal parry, catching the poisoned blade right against his crossguard. Purple corrosive smoke hisses where the venom touches the starlight-infused steel; Jin's golden-amber eyes blaze inches from the shadow-hound mask, and both sides of his face are completely smooth and unscarred.
**Dialogue:**
**SFX:** *SKREEEEE—HISS!*
Shadow-Hound Assassin: "The Starfall Blade... Even awakened, you're just a fledgling playing with a dead god's toy!"
Jin: "Funny—it cuts creeps in dog masks just fine!"

## Panel 42
**Visual:** Medium action shot as Jin twists his wrists, using the leverage of the Starfall Blade to disarm the assassin. The tip of the Starfall Blade slices across the side of the assassin's wooden shadow-hound mask, cracking the cheek of the mask in half and sending the poisoned dagger spinning off into the abyss below.
**Dialogue:**
**SFX:** *CRACK—CLATTER!*
Jin: "Now let's see whose face is under that bark!"

## Panel 43
**Visual:** Close-up on the assassin's exposed lower face—pale, branded with a black silence-tattoo across his lips—as he crushes a glass smoke-pellet strapped to his wrist guard. A blinding cloud of crimson and black abyssal smoke explodes in the cramped stairwell directly in Jin's face.
**Dialogue:**
**SFX:** *KRA-KOOM!*
Jin: "Ghk—! Abyssal smoke?!"

## Panel 44
**Visual:** Medium shot as Mira arrives on the landing below, raising her wooden mage staff to send a gust of clean teal wind spiraling up the stairwell, dispersing the black smoke around a coughing Jin. Above them, the assassin is already vaulting through a narrow ventilation embrasure that leads out toward the upper Academy grounds.
**Dialogue:**
Mira: "*Gale Purge!* Jin, are you hit?!"
Jin: "*Cough*—No, I parried the dagger! Look, up by the third landing—he's breaking through the outer grate!"

## Panel 45
**Visual:** Wide exterior shot looking out through the broken iron grate of the stairwell window into the moonlit night. The shadow-hound assassin leaps across the dizzying gap between the subterranean shaft tower and the buttresses of the tallest, darkest tower in the center of the campus—the High Council Spire—vanishing through a high balcony window.
**Dialogue:**
Jin (off-panel): "He didn't head for the outer walls..."
Mira (off-panel): "He fled straight into the High Council Spire."

## Panel 46
**Visual:** Medium two-shot inside the stairwell landing by the broken grate. Jin lowers the Starfall Blade, its glow fading back into the steel as Mira's teal ribbon settles against his gauntleted hand. His face is completely smooth and unscarred, though smudged slightly with soot on his chin, and his golden-amber eyes are fixed grimly on the distant High Council Spire through the bars. Beside him, Mira lowers her wooden mage staff, her silver-white hair catching the cold moonlight.
**Dialogue:**
Jin: "No outsider could bypass the spire's aerial wards like that. Archivist Orin was right. Whoever controls that assassin sits at the High Council table."
Mira: "And they already have the Obsidian Key from Northgate..."

## Panel 47
**Visual:** Close-up on Mira from the side. As Jin continues staring out the window on the viewer's left, Mira looks down at her own hand pressing over the front of her white-and-teal rune cloak, right where the silver Bell of Aethelgard is hidden against her heart. Her bright teal eyes are shadowed with secret fear and sorrow.
**Dialogue:**
Jin: "We won't let them open the Rifts, Mira. Whatever happens at Vaelen Temple or Northgate, my blade and your staff will stop them together. Promise me you won't do anything reckless on your own."
Mira: "...I promise, Jin. Together."

## Panel 48
**Visual:** Close-up of the stone steps at Jin's feet. Jin reaches down to pick it up.
**Dialogue:**

Mira: "That silver-and-obsidian thread... only the Inner Circle Elders wear robes woven with that pattern."

Behind him, the shadow-hound assassin kneels on one knee, holding his cracked mask against his chest. Elder Vane holds the actual Obsidian Key in his pale fingers—a jagged, tuning-fork-like artifact of pulsing black star-glass that drinks in the moonlight.
**Dialogue:**
Shadow-Hound Assassin: "Forgive me, Master. The boy's reflexes were faster than anticipated. I could not silence the Archivist."
Elder Vane: "It matters little. Orin is an old relic clinging to dust, and the boy's blade has awoken far too late."

## Panel 50
**Visual:** Extreme close-up on the Obsidian Key in Elder Vane's hand as dark crimson and violet abyssal energy arcs between its black glass prongs, reflecting in Elder Vane's cold, merciless eyes. In the far background below the spire, the distant lights of Starfall Academy glimmer like dying stars.
**Dialogue:**
Elder Vane: "Let the Astral Vanguard chase shadows in the archives. By the time they march on Vaelen Temple, the Obsidian Key will have turned the lock—and the Great Abyssal Rift will swallow the sky."
Narration: "Next Episode: The Shadow Wyrm's Fang."
