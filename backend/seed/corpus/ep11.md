# Episode 11 — Breaching the Black Causeway

## Panel 1
**Visual:** A sweeping, wide vertical establishing shot of the Black Causeway—an immense, jagged bridge of obsidian stone suspended over a churning sea of purple-black abyssal miasma. In the foreground, Jin sprints forward toward the towering silhouette of the Obsidian Citadel in the distance. He has tousled jet-black hair, fierce golden-amber eyes, and wears his high-collared dark navy astral coat with silver embroidery. Looking back slightly over his shoulder toward the camera, the bold crimson X-shaped blade scar is clearly visible on the viewer's left cheek (left side of the image canvas), while the viewer's right side of his face is completely smooth and unscarred. Clutched in his grip is the glowing Starfall Blade, with Mira's torn teal ribbon fluttering from the hilt. In the distant background, the stone spans of the causeway are already cracking and collapsing into the void as Instructor Kaelen and the armored knights of the Astral Vanguard hold a defensive line against a descending swarm of winged iron gargoyles. The sky above is fractured with violet lightning.
**Dialogue:**
Kaelen: "Go, Jin! Don't look back—we'll hold the bridge!"
Narration: "Beyond the Shattered Gorge lies the Black Causeway—the only artery feeding into the heart of Elder Vane's stronghold."

## Panel 2
**Visual:** Medium shot of Instructor Kaelen at the rear vanguard line, braced behind a massive kite shield etched with the silver crest of Starfall Academy. Kaelen is a broad-shouldered veteran with steel-gray hair, a braided beard, and heavy silver-and-cobalt plate armor scored with fresh claw marks. He swings his rune-etched greatsword in a wide horizontal arc, cleaving two diving iron gargoyles in half amidst a shower of orange sparks and black oil. Beside him, two Vanguard shield-bearers—Lieutenant Sera and Knight-Cadet Thorne—lock their glowing cyan barrier shields together against a hail of obsidian spears.
**Dialogue:**
Kaelen: "Vanguard! Lock overlapping Aegis formations! Do not let a single winged bastard slip past our flank!"
Sera: "Shields at seventy percent resonance, Commander! The abyssal pressure out here is chewing right through our mana reserves!"

## Panel 3
**Visual:** Close-up on Knight-Cadet Thorne, a young man in dented Astral Vanguard helm and pauldrons, gritting his teeth as the clawed feet of an iron gargoyle slam directly against his translucent cyan energy shield. Cracks of violet corruption spiderweb across the surface of his barrier. Behind him, a dozen more gargoyles with glowing red furnace-eyes drop from the dark storm clouds like iron meteors.
**Dialogue:**
Thorne: "They're heavier than the stone horrors at Vaelen Temple! Their hides are forged from solid abyssal slag!"
Kaelen: "Then hit them where the joints glow! Channel your astral aura into the seams!"

## Panel 4
**Visual:** Dynamic low-angle shot of Kaelen stomping his armored boot onto the cracked basalt paving stones of the bridge. A ring of brilliant cobalt-blue shockwaves erupts from his foot, launching three encroaching gargoyles backward off the edge of the causeway into the bottomless purple mist below. Kaelen's cape billows in the freezing gale as he glares toward the distant Citadel spires.
**Dialogue:**
Kaelen: "Elder Vane thinks a flock of enchanted scrap metal can stall the First Division?! We bought this bridge with blood—hold the line until Jin breaches that gate!"

## Panel 5
**Visual:** Tracking shot from the side as Jin sprints at breakneck speed down the central lane of the Black Causeway. His dark navy astral coat streams behind him, the silver embroidery catching the eerie violet lightning overhead. The Starfall Blade trails a comet-like ribbon of pale blue and silver starlight, while Mira's bright teal ribbon whips wildly in the slipstream around his knuckles. Beneath his boots, ancient runes carved into the obsidian roadway flare with hostile blood-red light, reacting to his presence.
**Dialogue:**
Narration: "Three hundred yards to the outer sanctum gate."
Jin (thinking): "Kaelen's unit is taking the brunt of the swarm. Every second I waste on this bridge is another pint of blood the Vanguard pays for me."

## Panel 6
**Visual:** Extreme close-up on Jin's face from the front as he runs into the howling headwind. His tousled jet-black hair whips across his forehead, and his golden-amber eyes burn with cold focus. On the viewer's left side of the canvas, the bold crimson X-shaped curse-scar on his cheek pulses with a faint, sinister heat in response to the Citadel's proximity; the viewer's right cheek remains completely smooth and unblemished.
**Dialogue:**
Jin (thinking): "The closer I get to the Citadel, the harder this scar burns. Elder Vane is inside... and the Obsidian Key is already resonating with the throne room."

## Panel 7
**Visual:** High-angle bird's-eye view of the Black Causeway. The bridge is a narrow black ribbon flanked by crumbling gothic gargoyle perches and rusted iron chains as thick as ancient oak trees. Halfway between Kaelen's defensive wall in the rear and the towering Citadel gate ahead, a massive section of the stone parapet suddenly explodes upward as subterranean chains snap beneath the masonry.
**Dialogue:**
Narration: "The causeway wasn't built to welcome armies. It was engineered as a guillotine."
Sera (off-panel, shouting from afar): "Jin! Watch the eastern pillars! The foundation runes are detonating!"

## Panel 8
**Visual:** Action shot of Jin leaping across a widening ten-foot chasm as an entire segment of the obsidian roadbed gives way, tumbling into the swirling purple abyss below. Mid-leap, Jin twists his torso, his dark navy coat flaring wide. Below him, jagged stalactites of black stone fall into the void, swallowed by glowing violet clouds.
**Dialogue:**
Jin: "Hup—!"

## Panel 9
