# Episode 12 — At the Threshold of Night

## Panel 1
**Visual:** Extreme vertical wide establishing shot that transitions in a scroll-down sweep into a tight front-facing medium close-up of Jin. He stands completely alone before the colossal, jagged black gates of the Obsidian Citadel. Behind him, the shattered remnants of the Black Causeway fall away into a bottomless chasm of swirling purple abyssal mist, leaving him cut off from all living allies. Above, a fractured sky of dark violet clouds is pierced by cold silver starlight. Jin has tousled jet-black hair wind-whipped across his brow and fierce golden-amber eyes glowing in the gloom. On the VIEWER'S LEFT cheek (the left side of the image canvas), his bold crimson X-shaped blade scar stands out starkly against his pale skin, while the viewer's right side of his face is completely smooth and unscarred. He wears his high-collared dark navy astral coat with silver embroidery, its hem tattered from the crossing. His hand rests on the hilt of the Starfall Blade at his hip, where Mira's frayed teal ribbon flutters in the freezing updraft.
**Dialogue:**
Jin (internal monologue): "Wait just a little longer, Mira. Tonight this ends."
Narration: "Beyond the fallen bridge, at the edge of the world where starlight goes to die—the Obsidian Citadel."

In the foreground, Jin's dark navy astral coat billows; the silver embroidery catches the pale starlight.
**Dialogue:**
